### One-workflow assisted pilot — research hypothesis

Indicative pricing to test after separately approved scope: **USD 1,500–3,000 per engagement**, with one workflow, initial intake, redaction guidance, first Evidence Pack preparation assistance, report walkthrough, evidence gaps and follow-up review. Not included: custom integration, code execution, source custody, runtime controls, legal advice, safety certification, production assurance or unlimited support.

### Evidence-based decision criteria

Evaluate with a target of 3–5 volunteers, not assumed recruits. Record unassisted completion, role comprehension, repeat intent, handoff usefulness and willingness to pay. After observed signals, propose KEEP / REVIEW / HOLD of the existing prices. Do not increase subscription pricing merely because the HTML renderer exists.

### Positioning

Value proposition: a local, reviewable, bounded evidence handoff for single-agent workflows, not a runtime governance control plane. Pilot measurements cannot establish generalized effectiveness or enterprise ROI without further evidence.
