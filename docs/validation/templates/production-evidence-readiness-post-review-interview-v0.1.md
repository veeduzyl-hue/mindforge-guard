# Production Evidence Readiness Post-Review Interview v0.1

Status: Interview template for a completed valid Experiment 2B review. Record responses as evidence, not assumptions.

## Review Ownership And Before State

1. Who owned the review, and what was their role?
2. What did the team do before Guard?
3. What was hardest before the review?
4. What evidence was still difficult to obtain?

## Guard Workflow Value

5. What changed with Guard?
6. Which findings were useful, and which were noise?
7. Did Guard reduce evidence chasing? Why or why not?
8. Did Guard reduce ambiguity in the real decision? Why or why not?
9. What was the reviewer disposition and what remained external to Guard?

## Repeat And Commercial Signals

10. Would the team use this again, and at what event?
11. Who would own budget and what is the procurement path?
12. Would the team consider a paid pilot? Record this as hypothesis or evidence.
13. What would prevent repeat use?
